# WHAA 2026 LinkedIn recap: final script and post (v11, 14 Nov 2026)

## Voiceover script (as recorded)

Where should healthcare place its next bold bet?

That question brought us back to The Wharton School of the University of Pennsylvania.

I first walked into Huntsman Hall at seventeen. Coming back, more than two decades and five countries later, still gave me goosebumps.

The opening keynote set the bar. Innovation isn't enough; a bold bet only pays off when the breakthrough reaches the patient.

Using HIV as a case study, leaders explored what it takes for payers, providers, industry and communities to move together and deliver on that promise.

On our Life Sciences AI panel, we asked AI to imagine healthcare in 2040: digital twins, robotic factories, and AI in the exam room.

Having worked across consulting, pharma and the World Health Organization, I've learned that technology is only half the bet.

The industry is betting billions on machines. My big bet is on human judgment.

Perhaps the real superintelligence isn't artificial at all. It's the human ability to ask better questions and turn breakthroughs into better outcomes for patients.

My bold bet? Experience across disciplines, and the human judgment to connect them.
