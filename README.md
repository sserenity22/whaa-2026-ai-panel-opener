# 2040, according to the AI

Opening film for Breakout Session #1C, *Leading the AI Transformation from Pipeline to Patient*, at the 2026 WHAA Annual Alumni Conference (Huntsman Hall, Friday 30 October 2026). Moderator: Christina Liu. Panelists: Nadine Besson, Subir Basak, Jin Ge, Jen Hinkel.

**Watch:** https://sserenity22.github.io/whaa-2026-ai-panel-opener/

The film is an imagined future: every headline, scene and announcement in it is fictional and labelled as such. None describes a real announcement, product, approval or plan by any company or person. Imagery and video were generated with FLUX 3 (Black Forest Labs); narration and music with ElevenLabs.
